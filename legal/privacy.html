1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account information: your email address, display name, password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider), profile photo and language.
  • Your content: tasks, subtasks, notes, pages, tags, dates, links you attach, time logs, habits, focus history, automation rules, custom fields and similar content you create.
  • Team content: when you join a team, your name, profile photo, role and the work assigned to you are visible to the other members of that team.
  • Client share pages: when you share a page, the visitors you send the link to can see the tasks you chose to share. Visitors who approve work or leave a note give us the name and text they type. If you allow it on a link, visitors can also attach images or PDF files; you can attach files to a share page as well. These files are stored privately and can only be opened through the share page with short-lived links.
  • Support messages: what you write to us, together with your email address, name and app version.
  • Waitlist: your email address if you join our waitlist.

Information from connected services (only if you connect them)

  • Calendars (Google Calendar, Microsoft Outlook, calendar subscription links): the events needed to show your busy times and to keep your tasks in sync. We write tasks only to a separate "TaskRack" calendar in your account.
  • Slack, email-to-task, GitHub, Jira, Asana, Trello, Todoist: the messages, emails or assigned items you choose to turn into tasks. Access tokens are encrypted and never shown to you again; assigned items from these tools are read live and not stored by us.
  • Voice assistants and our API: the commands you send with a key you create in the app.

Payment information

Payments are handled by our payment partners: Paddle (web and desktop), and Apple or Google through RevenueCat (mobile). They process your card details; we never see or store your full card number. We receive your plan, subscription status, renewal dates and country.

Usage analytics (only with your permission)

If you choose "Allow" when we ask, we record product events such as "task created" or "share page created", together with your user ID and the platform you use (for example, web or desktop). We never send task titles, notes, or your email address with these events. Analytics stays off until you allow it, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings.

Technical information

Our servers record standard technical information such as IP address, request time and error details, to keep the Service secure and working. We do not use it to build advertising profiles.

3. AI features

AI features (splitting a task into subtasks, project plans, the assistant, voice commands that the app cannot understand on its own) send the minimum content needed to an AI provider, currently Google (Gemini API). For example, the assistant receives your question and the titles, dates and priorities of your tasks; notes are not sent. We use the provider's paid API, under terms that do not allow it to use this content to train its models. We do not use your content to train AI models either. AI output can be wrong; please review suggestions before you apply them.

When you use "Tasks from a photo", the image you choose (reduced in size on your device first) is sent to the AI provider only to read the tasks in it. We do not store the image, and the provider's paid API terms do not allow it to be used for training. Avoid sending photos that show other people's sensitive information.

5. Cookies and device storage

The TaskRack app does not use advertising or tracking cookies. To work, it stores some information on your device (in your browser's local storage or the app's storage):

  • Strictly necessary: your sign-in session, your settings (language, theme), and an offline copy of your personal workspace so the app works without a connection.
  • Client share pages: the name a visitor types and their list/flow view choice are kept in that visitor's browser so they don't have to enter them again.
  • Analytics: only your yes/no choice about analytics is stored. Analytics events are sent only if you allowed them; no analytics cookie is set.

Our payment partner (Paddle) may set its own cookies on its checkout page to process payments and prevent fraud; see Paddle's own notice. We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals as an opt-out of analytics where the law requires it.

6. Retention

  • Your content is kept while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your content within 30 days, except for backups, which are overwritten within a further 30 days.
  • Files attached to client share pages are deleted automatically 90 days after upload, or sooner if you delete them or the share link. Images sent to read tasks are not stored.
  • Billing records are kept as long as tax and accounting laws require (generally up to 7 years).
  • Analytics events are kept for up to 24 months.
  • Server logs are kept for up to 90 days.
