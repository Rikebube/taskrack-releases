Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains how cancelling a TaskRack subscription works and how to get a refund. It is part of our Terms of Service. Nothing in this policy limits rights you have under the law of the country where you live.

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders on the web and desktop. Paddle provides all customer service inquiries and handles returns.

1. The short version

This section applies to purchases on the web and desktop. Purchases made on iPhone or Android follow Apple's or Google's refund rules (see section 4).

  • 14-day money-back guarantee on your first payment. You can request a full refund within 14 days of your first payment for a TaskRack plan. Each AI credit pack can also be refunded within 14 days of its purchase.
  • Renewals are not refunded. Later monthly or yearly renewal payments are not refundable.
  • You can cancel at any time. Cancelling stops the next renewal. Your paid plan keeps working until the end of the period you already paid for; the remaining days of that period are not refunded.

2. How to request a refund

Payments on the web and desktop are processed by Paddle. To request a refund, visit paddle.net and look up your purchase, or reply to your Paddle receipt email. You can also write to us at [SUPPORT EMAIL] and we will help. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days depending on your bank. Paddle's buyer terms also apply.

3. Cancelling

On the web and desktop you can cancel in the app's settings, or from the link in your Paddle receipt email. After cancellation your account moves to the free plan at the end of the paid period. Your content stays saved; features above the free plan's limits stop working until you subscribe again.

4. iPhone and Android

Subscriptions bought on iPhone or Android are sold by Apple or Google. Cancel them in your phone's subscription settings and request refunds from Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google (Google Play order history); their refund policies apply. Deleting your TaskRack account does not cancel a subscription bought through Apple or Google.

5. EEA and UK customers

If you live in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have a statutory right to withdraw from a purchase within 14 days. Our 14-day money-back guarantee on your first payment meets this right.

6. Payments between you and your clients

Payment links, payment steps and extra-revision fees shown on client share pages are between you and your client. TaskRack does not receive, hold or refund those payments.
